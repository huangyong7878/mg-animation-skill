**Language:** English

## Source notes

Wi-Fi uses radio waves to carry data. A router connects to the internet and
broadcasts a radio signal. A device with a Wi-Fi adapter picks up that signal
and exchanges data with the router. The 2.4 GHz band generally travels farther
and is more crowded; the 5 GHz band generally offers more speed at shorter
range.
