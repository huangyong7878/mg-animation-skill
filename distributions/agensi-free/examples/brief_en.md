## Source notes

A bill begins as an idea. A member of Congress drafts the text and introduces
it in one chamber. Committees hold hearings, amend the text and vote. The full
chamber then debates and votes. If both chambers pass identical text, it goes
to the president, who may sign it or veto it. Congress can override a veto
with a two-thirds vote in both chambers.
